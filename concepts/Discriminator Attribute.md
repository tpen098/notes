# Discriminator Attribute

## Definition

[[Discriminator Attribute|Discriminator Attributes]] are [[Primary Key|Primary Keys]] of an [[Identifying Entity Set|Identifying Set]] and additional [[Attribute|Attributes]], used to uniquely identify a [[Weak Entity Set]]. [^1]
