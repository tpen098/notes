## Definition

A [[Set]] $\mathcal{S}$ is an unordered, collection of distinct [[Set Membership|Elements]] or [[Set Membership|Member]] [^1]

## Notation

A [[Set]] $\mathcal{S}$ with $n$ distinct [[Set Membership|Elements]] can be denoted using a curly bracket and the list of [[Set Membership|Elements]]. separated via comma as shown [^1]

$$
\mathcal{S} = \{ s_1, s_2, s_3, \dots, s_{n}\}
$$
