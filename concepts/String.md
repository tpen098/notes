# String

## Definition

A [[String]] over a finite [[Set]] $S$ is a [[Finite Sequence]] of [[Set Membership|Elements]] of $S$ [^1]

> [!example]
> For example, given a binary [[Set]] $B = \{ 0, 1 \}$, all the possible [[String|Strings]] of length $3$ is the following:
>
> $$
> 000, 001, 010, 011, 100, 101, 110, 111 
> $$

## Programming Definition

A [[String]], as a [[Data Type]], consists of [[Finite Sequence]] of [[Character Type|Character Types]]. Depending on the [[Language|Programming Language]], it can be a special kind of [[Character Type|Character]] [[Array|Array]] or a [[Primitive Data Type]]. Another design consideration is whether the length of the [[String]] is static or dynamic. [^2]
