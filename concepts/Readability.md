# Readability

## Context

[[Readability]] is one of the most important criteria for judging a [[Language|Programming Language]]. It establishes the ease of reading and understanding [[Program|Programs]] [^1]

This criteria can be divided into four sections: [[Simplicity]], [[Orthogonality]], [[Data Type|Data Types]], and [[Syntax]] Design.
