# Data-Manipulation Language

The [[Data-Definition Language]] is provided by a [[Database-Management System]] to express [[Database]] [[Query Operation|Query Operations]] and [[Modifying Operation|Modifying Operations]]. [^1]

> [!note]
> Often, the [[Data-Definition Language]] and [[Data-Manipulation Language]] are not two separate [[Language|Languages]]. They instead form a single [[Database]] [[Language]]. [^1]
