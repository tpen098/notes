# Many-to-one Mapping Cardinality

## Definition

A [[Many-to-one Mapping Cardinality|Many-to-one]] [[Mapping Cardinality]] associates an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$ with at most one [[Entity|Entities]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$. However, an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$ can be associated with one or more [[Entity|Entities]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$. [^1]
