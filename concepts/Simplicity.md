# Simplicity

## Definition

A [[Language|Programming Language]] with a large number of basic constructs is more difficult to learn. This can lead to programmers only learning a subset of the [[Language]] and ignore the other features. This then leads to the case wherein [[Program|Programs]] can be difficult to understand if it relies on the subset of the [[Language|Programming Language]] unknown to the programmer. [^1]
