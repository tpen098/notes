# Span

## Definition

Consider a [[Vector Space]] $V = (\mathcal{V}, +, \cdot)$ and a [[Generating Set]] $\mathcal{A} = \{x_1, \ldots, x_k\} \subseteq \mathcal{V}$. The [[Set]] of all linear [[Combination|Combinations]] of [[Vector Space|Vectors]] in $\mathcal{A}$ is called the [[Span]] of $\mathcal{A}$.

$$
V = span[A]=span[x_1, \ldots, x_k]
$$
