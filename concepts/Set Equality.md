## Definition

The [[Set|Sets]] $\mathcal{S}_1, \mathcal{S}_2, ..., \mathcal{S}_n$ are considered [[Set Equality|equal]] if they [[Set Membership|contain]] the same [[Set Membership|Elements]] [^1]

$$
S_1 = S_2 = \dots = S_n
$$
