# Many-to-many Mapping Cardinality

## Definition

A [[Many-to-many Mapping Cardinality|Many-to-many]] [[Mapping Cardinality]] associates an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$ with one or more [[Entity|Entities]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$ and vice versa. [^1]
