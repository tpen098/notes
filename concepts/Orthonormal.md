# Orthonormal

## Statement

Two [[Vector Space|Vectors]], $x, y$ are [[Orthonormal]] if they are [[Orthogonal]] and if the [[Norm]] of each [[Vector Space|Vector]] equals one.

$$
\lVert x \rVert = \lVert y \rVert = 1
$$
