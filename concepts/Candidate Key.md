# Candidate Key

## Definition

A [[Candidate Key]] is a [[Superkey]] wherein it has been minimized to remove unnecessary keys to keep its identity as a [[Superkey]]. More formally, ut us a [[Superkey]] in which no proper [[Subset]] is a [[Superkey]]. [^1]
