# Dataset

## Definition

A [[Dataset]] $\mathcal{D}$ is a set of $N$ pairs of [[Model Input|Examples]] $x_{n} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ and their corresponding [[Model Label|Labels]] $y_n \in \mathbb{R}$ as shown

$$
\mathcal{D}_{train} = \{ (x_{1}, y_{1}), \dots, (x_{N}, y_{N}) \}
$$

> [!NOTE] Independent and Identically Distributed Assumption
> It is common to assume that the [[Dataset]] is [[Independent and Identically Distributed]]
