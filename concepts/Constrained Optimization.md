# Constrained Optimization

## Definition

Given a real-valued [[Function]] $f: \mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}$, [[Constrained Optimization]] is defined as the minimization of the [[Function]] $f$ with respect to its arguments $\theta$ while satisfying the condition $g_{i}(\theta) \leq 0, \forall i$.

$$
\min_{\theta} f(\theta), g_{i}(\theta) \leq 0,\forall i
$$
