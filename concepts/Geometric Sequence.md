## Definition

The [[Geometric Sequence]] is an [[Infinite Sequence]] wherein there is a [[concepts/Geometric Sequence|Common Ratio]] $r$ between a [[Function|Value]] $a_{i}$ and the previous [[Function|Value]] $a_{i-1}$ [^1]

$$
f(n) = f(n - 1) \cdot r \atop
\langle a_{1}, a_{1} \cdot r, a_{1} \cdot r^{2}, a_{1} \cdot r^{3}, \dots \rangle
$$

## Specific Value

If the [[Function|Domain]] is the [[Natural Number|Set of Natural Numbers]] $\mathbb{N}$, then the $n-th$ term is adjusted to the following [^1]

$$
a_{n} = a_{1} \cdot r^{n-1}
$$
