## Definition

The [[Left-Hand Limit]] is the [[Limit]]  of a [[Function]] $f(x)$ of as the [[Function|Argument]] $x$ approaches $a$ such that $x$ is sufficiently close to $a$ and $x$ is less than $a$ [^1]

$$
\lim_{ n \to a^{-} } f(x) = L
$$
