# Dimension

## Definition

The [[Dimension]] is the number of [[Basis|basis]] vectors of a finite-dimensional [[Vector Space]] $V$

$$ dim(V) $$

## Vector Space - Subspace Dimension

If $U$ is a [[Vector Subspace]] of $V$ then $dim(U) \leq dim(V)$ and $dim(U) = dim(V)$ if and only if $U = V$
