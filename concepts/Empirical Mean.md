# Empirical Mean

## Definition

The [[Empirical Mean]] is a [[Vector Space|Vector]] representing the arithmetic [[Mean|Average]] of the observations in the finite [[Dataset]] $n \in N$ for each variable $x_n(n) = [x_{1}(n), \dots, x_{D}(n)]$, defined as the following

$$
\bar{x} := \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^Nx_{n}
$$
