# Matrix Row Echelon Form

## Conditions

1. All zero rows appear at the bottom of the [[Matrix]]
2. For each nonzero row, the first nonzero entry or leading entry must be to the right of the preceding row's leading entry
3. The nonzero leading entry must be equal to one

> [!tip]
> An [[Upper Triangular Matrix]] is in [[Matrix Row Echelon Form]]. However, a [[Matrix]] in [[Matrix Row Echelon Form|REF]] can have fully zero rows or have zero diagonals.
