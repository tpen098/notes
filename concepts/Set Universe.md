## Definition

The [[Set Universe]] $U$ is the larger [[Set]] such that all of the other [[Set|Sets]] $\mathcal{S}_{1}, \mathcal{S}_{2}, \dots, \mathcal{S}_{n}$ is its [[Subset]]. Alternatively, the [[Set Universe]] can be defined as the [[Set Union]] of a [[Set]] $A$ and its [[Set Complement]] [^1]
