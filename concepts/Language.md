# Language

## Definition

A [[Language]] $L$ is a [[Set]] of [[String|Strings]] from a determined Finite [[Set]] known as an [[Language|Alphabet]] $\Sigma$ [^1]

$$
L \subseteq \Sigma^{*}
$$
