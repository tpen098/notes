## Definition

The [[Rational Number|Set of Rational Numbers]] is the [[Set]] consisting of numbers $r$ that can be expressed as a ratio of [[Integer|Integers]] [^1][^2]

$$
\mathbb{Q} = \left\{  r: r = \frac{m}{n}, m, n \in \mathbb{Z}, n \neq 0 \right\}
$$
