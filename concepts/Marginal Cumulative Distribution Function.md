# Marginal Cumulative Distribution Function

## Definition

Given [[Random Variable|Random Variables]] $X = \{ X_{1}, \dots, X_{n} \} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, of a Continuous [[Target Space]], the _Marginal [[Cumulative Distribution Function]]_ of $X_{i}$ with state $x_i \in \mathcal{T}_{i}$ is defined as the [[Probability]] of $X_{i} \leq x_{i}$ irrespective of the other [[Random Variable|Random Variables]]

$$
p(x_{i}) = P(X_{i} \leq x_{i})
$$
