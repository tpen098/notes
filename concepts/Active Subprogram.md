# Active Subprogram

## Definition

A [[Subprogram]] is considered [[Active Subprogram|Active]] if, after its [[Subprogram Call]], it has begun execution but has not yet completed said execution [^1]

[1]: Concepts of Programming Languages, Ch. 9, p. 391
