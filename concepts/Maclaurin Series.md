# Maclaurin Series

## Definition

The [[Maclaurin Series]] is a special instance of the [[Taylor Series]] wherein $x_0 = 0$.

$$
M_{{\infty}}(x) := \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(k)}(0)}{k!}(x)^k
$$
