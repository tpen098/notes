## Definition

The [[Absolute Value]] of a [[Real Number]] $a$ is defined as the following [[concepts/Expression]] [^1]

$$
\lvert a \rvert =
\begin{cases}
+a & a \geq 0 \\
-a & a \lt 0
\end{cases}
$$

## Properties

### Always Positive

The [[Absolute Value]] of a [[Real Number]] is always positive or zero [^1]

$$
\lvert a \rvert \geq 0
$$

### Sign Indifference

Additionally, a [[Real Number]] and its negation $-a$ have the same [[Absolute Value]] [^1]

$$
\lvert a \rvert = \lvert -a \rvert 
$$

### Product and Quotient Application

The [[Absolute Value]] of a product is the product or quotient of the [[Absolute Value|Absolute Values]] [^1]

$$
\lvert ab \rvert = \lvert a \rvert \lvert b \rvert \atop
\left\lvert \dfrac{a}{b} \right\rvert  = \dfrac{\lvert a \rvert }{\lvert b \rvert }
$$

### Triangle Inequality

The [[Absolute Value]] of the sum of [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $a, b$ is less than or equal to the sum of the [[Absolute Value|Absolute Values]] [^1]

$$
\lvert a + b \rvert \leq \lvert a \rvert + \lvert b \rvert 
$$

## Modulus

The [[Absolute Value|Modulus]] or [[Absolute Value]] of a [[Complex Number]] $z = a + bi$ is the [[Root|Square Root]] of the sum of the terms, each with [[Exponent]] $2$ as shown [^1]

$$
\lvert z \rvert = \sqrt{ a^{2} + b^{2} }
$$
