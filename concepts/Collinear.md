# Collinear

## Definition

Two [[Vector Space|Vectors]] $x_1, x_2$ are [[Collinear]] if one is a scalar multiple of another via [Matrix Scaling](Matrix%20Scaling.md) and has the same direction (sign).

$$
x_1 = \lambda x_2 \text{ or } x_1 = -\lambda x_2, \lambda \in \mathbb{R}
$$
