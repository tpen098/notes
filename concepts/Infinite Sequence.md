## Definition

An [[Infinite Sequence]] is a [[Function]] $f$ whose [[Function|Domain]] is usually the [[Natural Number|Natural Numbers]] (or [[Whole Number|Whole Numbers]] for $0$-indexing) [^1]

$$
\{ 1, 2, 3, \dots \} \to \langle f(1), f(2), f(3), \dots \rangle \atop
\{ 0, 1, 2, \dots \} \to \langle f(0), f(1), f(2), \dots \rangle
$$
