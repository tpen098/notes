# Injection

## Definition

A [[Function]] $f: A \to B$ is an [[Injection]] if distinct [[Function|Arguments]] $a \in A$ produce distinct [[Function|Values]] $b \in B$ [^1]

$$
a \neq a' \implies f(a) \neq f(a')
$$

> [!example]
> The [[Function]] $f(n) = 2n$ is an [[Injection|Injective]] [[Function]] from $\mathbb{N}$ to $\mathbb{N}$ since each [[Function|Value]] $b$ is an [[Image]] under $f$ of at most one [[Function|Argument]] $\dfrac{b}{2}$
