## Definition

A [[Binary Relation]] is a [[Partial Order]] if it is [[Reflexive Relation|Reflexive]], [[Antisymmetric Relation|Antisymmetric]], and [[Transitive Relation|Transitive]] [^1]

> [!example]
> An example of a [[Partial Order]] is the operation "is a descendent of" assuming an [[Set Membership|Element]] can be a descendent of itself.
