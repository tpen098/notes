# Linear System General Solution

## Definition

The [[Linear System General Solution]] of a [[Linear System]] in [[Matrix]] form is the Variable Column Vector that solves $Ax = 0$.

$$
\lambda_1
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{11} \\ x_{12} \\ \vdots \\ x_{1m}
\end{bmatrix}
\lambda_2
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{21} \\ x_{22} \\ \vdots\\ x_{2m}
\end{bmatrix}
\ldots
\\
\lambda_m
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{m1} \\ x_{m2} \\ \vdots\\ x_{mn}
\end{bmatrix}

$$

> [!tip] Common Shortcut
> The [[Minus 1 Trick]] is often used to determine the vectors.
