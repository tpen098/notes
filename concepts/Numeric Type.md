# Numeric Type

## Definition

The [[Numeric Type]] is a [[Primitive Data Type]] to represent numbers, often varying according to size and being signed or unsigned. There are three commonly implemented [[Numeric Type|Numeric Types]]: [^1]
- [[Integer|Integers]]
- [[Rational Number]] as Floating-Point Numbers (approximated)
- [[Complex Number|Complex Numbers]] (pair of Floating-Point Numbers)

> [!NOTE]
> The most common [[Primitive Data Type]] is the [[Integer]] [[Data Type]]
