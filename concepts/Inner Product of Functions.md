# Inner Product of Functions

## Definition

An [[Inner Product]] of two [[Function|Functions]], $u: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ and $v: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ can be defined as the following [[Definite Integral]] for finite limits $[a, b]$:

$$
\langle u, v \rangle := \int_a^b u(x) v(x) dx
$$
