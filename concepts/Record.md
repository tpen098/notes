# Record

## Definition

A [[Record]] is a mapping or a [[Function]] from an [[Function|Input]] known as a [[Record|Key]] or [[Record|Field]] to a [[Function|Output]] or [[Record|Satellite Data]] [^1] A [[Record]] can be an aggregate of elements with different [[Data Type|Data Types]] .[^2]
