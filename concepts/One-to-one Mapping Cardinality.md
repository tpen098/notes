# One-to-one Mapping Cardinality

## Definition

A [[One-to-one Mapping Cardinality|One-to-one]] [[Mapping Cardinality]] associates an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$ with at most one other [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$, and vice versa [^1]
