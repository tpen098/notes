# One-to-many Mapping Cardinality

## Definition

A [[One-to-many Mapping Cardinality|One-to-many]] [[Mapping Cardinality]] associates an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$ with one or more [[Entity|Entities]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$. However, an [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $B$ is associated with only at most one [[Entity]] in [[Entity Set]] $A$. [^1]
