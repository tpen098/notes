# Sample Space

## Definition

The [[Sample Space]], $\Omega$ is the [[Set]] of all possible outcomes

## Example

For two successive coin tosses, the [[Sample Space]] is the following

$$
\Omega = \{ hh, tt, ht, th \}
$$
