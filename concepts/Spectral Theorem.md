# Spectral Theorem

## Statement

If a [[Square Matrix]] $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is a [Symmetric Matrix](Matrix%20Special%20Types.md#Symmetric%20Matrix), then there exists an [Orthonormal Basis](Orthonormal%20Basis.md) of the corresponding [Vector Space](Vector%20Space.md) $V$ consisting of [Eigenvectors](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) of $A$ and each [Eigenvalue](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) is real.
