## Definition

A [[Function]] $f(n)$ is [[Polynomially Bounded]] if its [[Asymptotic Upper Bound]] is $n$ raised to a constant [[Exponent|Power]] $k$ [^1]

$$
f(n) = O(n^{k})
$$
