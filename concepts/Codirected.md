# Codirected

## Definition

Two [[Vector Space|Vectors]] $x_1, x_2$ are codirected if they are [Collinear](Collinear.md) and have the same direction or sign as the other.

$$
x_1 = \lambda x_2, \lambda \in \mathbb{R}
$$
