# Target Space

## Definition

The [[Target Space]] $\mathcal{T}$ is the set of all possible outcomes of a quantity of interest. The components of the [[Target Space]] are often called [[Target Space|State]].

## Example

For two successive coin tosses, a possible [[Target Space]] can be the number of $h$ outcomes as shown in the following

$$
\mathcal{T} = \{ 0, 1, 2 \}
$$

## Probability

For any subset $S \subseteq \mathcal{T}$, a [[Probability]] $P_{X}(S)$ is associated to a particular event occurring that corresponds to the [[Random Variable]] $X$
