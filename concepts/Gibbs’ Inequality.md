# Gibbs' Inequality

## Statement

The [[Gibbs’ Inequality]] notes that the [[Kullback–Leibler Divergence|Relative Entropy]] is always greater or equal to zero

$$
D_{KL}(P \mid\mid Q) \geq 0
$$
