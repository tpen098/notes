## Definition

A [[Finite Sequence]] of length $n$ is a [[Function]] $f$ whose [[Function|Domain]] is usually the [[Subset]] of [[Natural Number|Natural Numbers]] (or [[Whole Number|Whole Numbers]] for $0$-indexing) [^1]

$$
\{ 1, 2, \dots, n \} \to \langle f(1), f(2), \dots, f(n) \rangle \atop
\{ 0, 1, \dots, n-1 \} \to \langle f(0), f(1), \dots, f(n-1) \rangle 
$$
