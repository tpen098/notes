## Definition

For any [[Real Number]] $x$, the [[Ceiling]] is an [[Expression]] $\lceil x \rceil$ defined as the smallest [[Integer]] greater than or equal to $x$ [^1]

## Properties

### Inversion to Floor

Given a [[Real Number]] $x$, the negative of a [[Ceiling]] is the [[Floor]] of the negative of $x$ [^1]

$$
- \lceil x \rceil = \lfloor - x \rfloor 
$$

### Exclusion

Given [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $x, n$, the [[Ceiling]] $\lceil n + x \rceil$ can be simplified to $n$ plus the [[Ceiling]] of $x$ [^1]

$$
\lceil n+x \rceil = n + \lceil x \rceil  
$$
