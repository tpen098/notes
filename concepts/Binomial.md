## Definition

A [[Binomial]] is an [[concepts/Expression]] consisting of the addition of two [[Monomial|Monomials]] with the same [[Variable]] $x$ and two constant [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $a_{1}, a_{0}$ as shown [^1]

$$
a_{1} x^{k_{1}} + a x^{k_{0}}
$$
