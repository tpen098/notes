# Control Statement

## Definition

A [[Control Statement]] is a [[Statement]] that provides the means to select among alternative control flow paths or [[Selection Statement|Selection Statements]] and means of causing repeated execution of [[Statement|Statements]] or sequences of [[Statement|Statements]] or [[Iterative Statement|Iterative Statements]] [^1]
