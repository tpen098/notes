# Event Space

## Definition

The [[Event Space]] is the space of potential results of the experiment. Alternatively, it is the collection of [[Subset|Subsets]] of $\Omega$.

A [[Subset]] $A$ of the [[Sample Space]] called an [[Event Space|Event]] is in the [[Event Space]] $\mathcal{A}$ if, at the end of the experiment, it can be observed if a particular [[Sample Space|Outcome]] $\omega \in \Omega$.

> [!NOTE] Discrete Probability Distributions
> For Discrete Probability Distributions, the [[Event Space]] $\mathcal{A}$ is often the [[Power Set]] of the [[Sample Space]] $\Omega$.
