# Divergent

## Definition

If the [[Limit]] of the [[Partial Sum]] as $n$ approaches infinity is the finite [[Function|Value]] $c$, then the [[Infinite Series]] is [[Divergent]][^1]

$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } S \to \infty
$$
