## Definition

The [[Real Number|Set of Real Numbers]] $\mathbb{R}$ is the [[Set]] that includes the [[Rational Number|Set of Rational Numbers]], [[Irrational Number|Set of Irrational Numbers]], [[Integer|Set of Integers]], [[Whole Number|Set of Whole Numbers]], and [[Natural Number|Set of Natural Numbers]] [^1]
