# Power Series

## Definition

The [[Power Series]] is a representation of a [[Function]], defined as the following

$$
f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty a_{k}(x-c)^k
$$

Wherein $a_k$ are _coefficients_ and $c$ is a constant.
