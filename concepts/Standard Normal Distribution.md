# Standard Normal Distribution

## Definition

A [[Standard Normal Distribution]] is a [[Gaussian Distribution|Normal Distribution]] with a zero [[Mean]] vector $\mu=0$ and a [[Covariance|Covariance Matrix]] to itself equal to the [[Identity Matrix]] $\Sigma = I$ ([[Standard Deviation]] is one for univariate case $\sigma=1$)
