# Euler's Number

## Definition

The constant [[Euler's Number]] $e$ is defined as the [[Limit]] of $\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{n}$ as $n$ approaches infinity [^1]

$$
e 
= \lim_{ n \to \infty } \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{n}
\approx 2.71828182845904523536
$$
