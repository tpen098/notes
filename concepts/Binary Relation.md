# Binary Relation

## Definition

A [[Binary Relation]] $R$ on two [[Set|Sets]] $A, B$ is a [[Subset]] of the [[Cartesian Product]] $A \times B$ [^1]

> [!NOTE] Notation
> Given a [[Tuple]] $(a, b)$ and [[Binary Relation]] $R$, the [[Set Membership|Set Containment]] $(a, b) \in R$ can also be written as $a \; R \; b$
