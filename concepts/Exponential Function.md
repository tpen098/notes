# Exponential Function

## Definition

The [[Exponential Function]] is a [[Function]] that takes a [[Variable]] [[Function|Argument]] $x$ and returns the [[Exponent]] of [[Exponent|Base]] $a$ raised to $x$ as shown [^1]

$$
f(x) = a^{x}, a \gt 0, a \neq 1
$$

> [!note] Natural Exponential Function
> A common definition of the [[Exponential Function]] usually uses the [[Euler's Number|Euler Number]] $e$ as the [[Exponent|Base]] to define the [[Exponential Function|Natural Exponential Function]] (which is often just referred to as the [[Exponential Function]])[^1]
>
> $$
> f(x) = e^{x}
> $$
