## Notation

An [[Set Membership|Element]] $s$ that is a part of a [[Set]] $\mathcal{S}$ is denoted using the following notation [^1]

$$
s \in \mathcal{S}
$$

Conversely, an [[Set Membership|Element]] $s$ that is not a part of a [[Set]] $\mathcal{S}$ is denoted with the following [^1]

$$
s \not\in S
$$
