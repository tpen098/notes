## Definition

A [[Polynomial]] of [[Variable]] $x$ is an [[concepts/Expression|Algebraic Expression]] that is a sum of more than three [[Monomial|Monomials]] with the highest [[Exponent|Power]] being $n$, called the [[Polynomial|Degree]] [^1]

$$
a_{n} x^{n} + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \dots + a_{1} x^{1} + a_{0} x^{0} 
$$
