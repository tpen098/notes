# Random Variable Inner Product

## Definition

Given two [[Random Variable|Random Variables]], $X, Y$ with zero [[Correlation]] and both having zero [[Mean|Means]], the [[Inner Product]] of the two [[Random Variable|Random Variables]] can be defined as their [[Covariance]]

$$
\langle X, Y \rangle := Cov[x, y]
$$
