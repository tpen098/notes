# Foreign Key

## Definition

Given a [[Set]] of [[Attribute|Attributes]] $A$ called [[Foreign Key|Foreign Keys]], the values of $A$ for each [[Tuple]] in [[Relational Data Model|Relation]] $r_{1}$ must also be values of [[Primary Key|Primary Keys]] $B$ of [[Relational Data Model|Relation]] $r_{2}$. In this case, $r_{1}$ is called the [[Foreign Key|Referencing Relation]] and $r_{2}$ is the [[Foreign Key|Referenced Relation]]. [^1]
