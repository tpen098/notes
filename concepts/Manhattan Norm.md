# Manhattan Norm

## Definition

The [[Manhattan Norm]] is a [[Norm]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is defined for $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ as the following

$$
\lVert x \rVert_1 = \sum_{i=1}^n \lvert x_i \rvert
$$
