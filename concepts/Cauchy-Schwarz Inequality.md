# Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

## Statement

For an [[Inner Product#Inner Product Space|Inner Product Space]] $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$, the induced [[Norm]] $\lVert \cdot \rVert$ satisfies the following inequality

$$
\lvert \langle x, y \rangle \rvert
\leq
\lVert x \rVert \lVert y \rVert
$$
