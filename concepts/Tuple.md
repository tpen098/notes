# Tuple

## Definition

A [[Tuple]] $\mathcal{T}$ is an ordered [[Set]] of [[Set Membership|Elements]] $(t_{1}, t_{2}, \dots, t_{n})$, formally defined as a [[Set Collection]] of [[Set|Sets]] that include each [[Set Membership|Element]] incrementally as shown [^1]

$$
(t_{1}, t_{2}, \dots, t_{n}) = 
\{ 
t_{1}, \{ t_{1}, t_{2} \}, \{ t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3} \}, \dots
\}
$$

> [!NOTE]
> A common [[Tuple]] is a [[Tuple]] with two elements $(a, b)$ called an [[concepts/Tuple|Ordered Pair]]

A [[Tuple]] is similar to a [[Record]] except the elements are not named. [^2]
