## Definition

A [[Half-Open Interval]] is an interval of the [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] that is open (exclusive) on one endpoint and closed (inclusive) on the other endpoint [^1]

$$
\begin{align}
(a, b] &= \{ x \in \mathbb{R}: a \lt x \leq b \} \\
[a, b) &= \{ x \in \mathbb{R}: a \leq x \lt b \} \\
\end{align}
$$
