# Angle

## Statement

Assume $x \neq 0, y \neq 0$. Using the [[Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality]], the following holds:

$$
-1
\leq
\dfrac{\langle x, y \rangle}
	  {\lVert x\rVert \lVert y\rVert}
\leq
1
$$

Therefore, there exists a unique [[Variable]], called [[Angle]], $\omega \in [ 0, \pi ]$ between [[Vector Space|Vectors]] $x, y$

$$
\cos{\omega}
=
\dfrac{\langle x, y \rangle}
	  {\lVert x\rVert \lVert y\rVert}
$$
