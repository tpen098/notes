# Closed Interval

## Definition

A [[Closed Interval]] $[a, b]$ is the [[Set]] of [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] between [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $a, b$, inclusive of these bounds [^1]

$$
[a, b] = \{ x \in \mathbb{R}: a \leq x \leq b \}
$$
