## Definition

A [[Function]] is a [[Surjection]] if its [[Range]] is its [[Function|Codomain]] [^1]

$$
B = f(A)
$$

> [!example]
> The [[Function]] $f(n) = \left\lfloor  \frac{n}{2}  \right\rfloor$  is a [[Surjection|Surjective]] [[Function]] from $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ since every element of $\mathbb{N}$ appears as a [[Function|Value]] of $f$
