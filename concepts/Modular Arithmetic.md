## Definition

For any [[Integer]] $a$, and any positive [[Integer]] $n$, the [[Modular Arithmetic]] $a \text{ mod } n$ results in the remainder of the quotient $\dfrac{a}{n}$ . It is also defined as $a$ minus the product of $n$ and the [[Floor]] of $\dfrac{a}{n}$ [^1]

$$
a \text{ mod } n = a - n \left\lfloor  \frac{a}{n}  \right\rfloor 
$$
