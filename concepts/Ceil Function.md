## Definition

The [[Ceil Function|Ceiling Function]] is a [[Function]] that takes an [[Variable]] [[Function|Argument]] $x$ and returns the [[Ceiling|Ceil]] of $x$ [^1]

$$
f(x) = \lceil x \rceil 
$$
