# Linear Mapping

## Statement

For [[Vector Space|vector spaces]] $V, W$, a mapping $\Phi: V \rightarrow W$ is called a [[Linear Mapping]] if the following holds:

$$
\forall x, y \in V, \forall \lambda, \psi \in \mathbb{R}:
\Phi(\lambda x + \psi y) = \lambda \Phi(x) + \psi \Phi(y)
$$
