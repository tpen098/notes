## Definition

Given two [[Set|Sets]] $A, \mathcal{S}$, if all the [[Set Membership|Elements]] of $A$ are in $\mathcal{S}$, then $A$ is a [[Subset]] of $\mathcal{S}$ [^1]

$$
A \subseteq \mathcal{S}: \{ a| a \in \mathcal{S} \}
$$
