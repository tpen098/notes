## Definition

A [[Quadratic Equation]] is a second [[Polynomial|Degree]] [[Polynomial]] [[Equation]] of [[Variable]] $x$ with constants $a \neq 0, b, c$ for decreasing [[Exponent|Powers]] of $x$ as shown [^1]

$$
a x^{2} + bx + c = 0
$$

### Solution

The [[Equation|Solution]] of a [[Quadratic Equation]] $a x^2 + b x + c = 0 \; (a \neq 0)$, is defined using the [[Quadratic Equation]] as shown [^1]

$$
x = \frac{{-b \pm \sqrt{ b_{2} - 4ac }}}{2a}
$$

## Discriminant

Given a general [[Quadratic Equation]] $a x^2 + b x + c = 0 \; (a \neq 0)$, the [[Quadratic Equation|Discriminant]] is the term inside the [[Root|Square Root]] of the [[Quadratic Equation]] that dictates the nature of the [[Equation|Solutions]] [^1]

$$
D = b^{2} - 4 ac
$$

 - If the [[Quadratic Equation|Discriminant]] is greater than zero, then it has two distinct, [[Real Number|Real]] [[Equation|Solutions]]
 - If the [[Quadratic Equation|Discriminant]] is exactly zero, then it has two similar (or simply one) [[Real Number|Real]] [[Equation|Solution]]
 - If the [[Quadratic Equation|Discriminant]] is less than zero, then it has a [[Complex Number|Complex]] [[Equation|Solution]]
