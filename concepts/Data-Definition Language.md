# Data-Definition Language

## Definition

The [[Data-Definition Language]] is provided by the [[Database-Management System]] to specify the [[Schema|Database Schema]] and specify additional properties of the data [^1]

> [!note]
> Often, the [[Data-Definition Language]] and [[Data-Manipulation Language]] are not two separate [[Language|Languages]]. They instead form a single [[Database]] [[Language]]. [^1]
