# Cosine Power Series Representation

## Definition

The $\cos(x)$ [[Function]] can be represented using the following [[Power Series]] representation

$$
\cos(x) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty(-1)^k \frac{1}{(2k)!}x^{2k}
$$
