# Mean

## Definition

The [[Mean]] of a multivariate [[Random Variable]] $X \sim p(x)$ is defined as its [[Expected Value|Expectation]] if $g(x_{i}) = x_{i}$, as shown in the following [[Matrix]]

$$
\mathbb{E}_{X}[x] =
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbb{E}_{X_{1}}[x_{1}] \\
\vdots \\
\mathbb{E}_{X_{D}}[x_{D}] \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
