## Definition

The [[Maximum Operation]] $MAXIMUM(S)$ is a [[Query Operation]] on a [[Total Order|Totally Ordered]] [[Data Structure]] that returns a [[Pointer]] to the largest [[Set Membership|Element]] $x$ [^1]
