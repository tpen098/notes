# Homomorphism

## Description

Given [[Group|Groups]] $V, W$, a [[Function]] $\Phi: V \rightarrow W$ with binary operation $\cdot$ is on the [[Function|Domain]] of $V$ and binary operation $\circ$ is on the [[Function|Codomain]] of $W$, the [[Function]] $\Phi$i s a [[Homomorphism]] if it satisfies the following:

$$
\Phi(v \cdot w) = \Phi(v) \circ \Phi(w), \forall v \in V, w \in W
$$
