## Definition

The [[Whole Number|Set of Whole Numbers]] is the [[Set]] of [[Natural Number]] including the number zero [^1]

$$
\mathbb{W} = \{ 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots \}
$$

> [!note] Difference with Whole Number
> In some text, the [[Natural Number|Set of Natural Numbers]] includes the number zero as well but use the version that exclude it and define the [[Whole Number|Set of Whole Numbers]] in case it's necessary.
