## Definition

The [[Arithmetic Series]] is the [[Partial Sum]] of an [[Arithmetic Sequence]] up to $n$ [[Function|Values]] [^1]

$$
\begin{align}
S 
&= \sum_{k=1}^{n} [a_{1} + (k-1)d] \\
&= \frac{n}{2} (2a_{1} + (n-1)d) \\
&= n\left( \frac{{a_{1} + a_{n}}}{2} \right) \\
\end{align}
$$
