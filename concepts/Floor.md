# Floor

## Definition

For any [[Real Number]] $x$, the [[Floor]] $\lfloor x \rfloor$ is an [[concepts/Expression]] or [[Function]], defined as the largest [[Integer]] less than or equal to $x$ is the [^1]

## Properties

### Inversion to Floor

Given a [[Real Number]] $x$, the negative of a [[Floor]] is the [[Ceiling|Ceil]] of the negative of $x$ [^1]

$$
-\lfloor x \rfloor = \lceil - x \rceil
$$

### Exclusion

Given [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $x, n$, the [[Floor]] $\lfloor n+x \rfloor$ can be simplified to $n$ plus the [[Floor]] of $x$ [^1]

$$
\lfloor n + x \rfloor  = n + \lfloor x \rfloor 
$$
