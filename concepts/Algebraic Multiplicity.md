# Algebraic Multiplicity

## Definition

Let a [[Square Matrix]] $A$ have an [Eigenvalue](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) $\lambda_i$, then the [[Algebraic Multiplicity]] of the [[Eigenvalue Equation|Eigenvalue]] is the number of times the [[Equation|Root]] appears in the [Characteristic Polynomial](Characteristic%20Polynomial.md).
