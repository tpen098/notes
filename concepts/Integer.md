# Integer

## Definition

The [[Integer|Set of Integers]] is the [[Set]] of positive and negative [[Natural Number|Natural Numbers]], and the number zero [^1][^2]

$$
\mathbb{Z} = \{ \dots, -2, -1, 0, +1, +2, \dots \}
$$
