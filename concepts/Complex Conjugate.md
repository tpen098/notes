# Complex Conjugate

## Definition

For a given [[Complex Number]] $z = a + bi$, the [[Complex Conjugate]] $\bar{z}$ is an [[concepts/Expression]] with the same result, except the [[Complex Number|Imaginary Part]] is negated [^1]

$$
z = a \pm bi \atop
\bar{z} = a \mp bi
$$

Note that multiplying the [[Complex Number]] $z$ with its [[Complex Conjugate]] $\bar{z}$ results in the following [[concepts/Expression]] [^1]

$$
z \cdot \bar{z} = a^{2} + b^{2} 
$$
