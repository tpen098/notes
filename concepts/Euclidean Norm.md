# Euclidean Norm

## Definition

The [[Euclidean Norm]] is a [[Norm]] of $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is defined as the following

$$
\lVert x \rVert_2 =
\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2 } = \sqrt{x^{\top}x}
$$

> [!NOTE]
> The [[Euclidean Norm]] can also be interpreted as the square root of the vector's [[Dot Product]] with itself.
