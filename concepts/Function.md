## Definition

A [[Function]] $f$ is a [[Binary Relation]] on [[Set|Sets]] $A, B$, called [[Function|Domain]] and [[Function|Codomain]] respectively, such that for all $a \in A$ there exists precisely one $b \in B$ such that $(a, b) \in f$ [^1]

$$
\begin{align}
f &: A \to B \\
(a, b) & \in f, b = f(a)
\end{align}
$$

> [!NOTE]
> The [[Function]] $f$ assigns an [[Set Membership|Element]] of the [[Function|Codomain]] or [[Function|Value]] $b \in B$ with an [[Set Membership|Element]] of the [[Function|Domain]] or [[Function|Argument]] $a \in A$. This means no [[Function|Argument]] is assigned to more than one [[Function|Value]] but a [[Function|Value]] can be assigned to more than one [[Function|Argument]].

## Equality

Two [[Function|Functions]] $f, g$ are considered equal if they have the same [[Function|Domain]] and [[Function|Codomain]] such that $\forall a, f(a) = g(a)$
