# Composite Attribute

## Definition

A [[Composite Attribute]] are [[Attribute|Attributes]] that can be further divided into subpart [[Attribute|Attributes]]. [^1]

> [!EXAMPLE] Common Example
> An example of a [[Composite Attribute]] is a `Name` that can be further divided into `Given Name`, `Middle Name`, and `Last Name`.
