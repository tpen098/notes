# Geometric Multiplicity

## Definition

Let a [[Square Matrix]] $A$ have an [Eigenvalue](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) $\lambda_i$, then the geometric multiplicity of $\lambda_i$ is the number of [Linearly Independent](Linear%20Independence.md) [Eigenvectors](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) associated with $\lambda_i$.

## Minimum Geometric Multiplicity

An [Eigenvalue](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md)'s [Geometric Multiplicity](Geometric%20Multiplicity.md) must at least be one
