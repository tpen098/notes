## Definition

The [[Factorial]] of a positive [[Integer]] $n \geq 0$ is recursively defined as $n$ times the [[Factorial]] of $n-1$ until $n=0$ wherein the its [[Factorial]] is constantly defined as one [^1]

$$
n = \begin{cases}
1 & n = 0 \\
n \cdot (n-1)! & n \gt 0
\end{cases}
$$
