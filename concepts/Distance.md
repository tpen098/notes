# Distance

## Statement

Consider an [[Inner Product#Inner Product Space|Inner Product Space]] $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$. The following is the distance between $x, y \in V$

$$
d(x, y)
:=
\lVert x - y \rVert
=
\sqrt{\langle x - y, x - y \rangle}
$$

> [!Note]
> If the [[Dot Product]] is used as the [[Inner Product]], then the distance is called [[Distance|Euclidean Distance]].
