# Set Complement

i

## Definition

Given a [[Set Universe]] $U$ and [[Set]] $A$, the [[Set Complement]] $\bar{A}$ is the [[Set Difference]] of the [[Set Universe]] $U$ and the [[Set]] $A$ [^1]

$$
\bar{A} 
= U - A  
= \{ x: x \in U, x \not\in A \}
$$

> [!tip] Simpler Terms
> The [[Set Complement]] is basically all the [[Set Membership|Elements]] that are not in the original [[Set]] $A$. A [[Set Universe]] is given since it's a referential value.

## Double Complement

Applying the [[Set Complement]] to a [[Set Complement]] is the original [[Set]] [^1]

$$
\bar{\bar{A}} = A
$$
