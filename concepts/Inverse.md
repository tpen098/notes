## Definition

Given a [[Bijection|Bijective]] [[Function]] $f: A \to B$, the [[Inverse]] $f^{-1}$ is defined as the [[Function]] $f^{-1}: B \to A$ [^1]

$$
f^{-1}(b) = a \iff f(a) = b
$$
