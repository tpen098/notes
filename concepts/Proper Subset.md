## Definition

Given two [[Set|Sets]] $A, \mathcal{S}$, the [[Set]] $A$ is a [[Proper Subset]] of $\mathcal{S}$ if $A$ is a [[Subset]] but not [[Set Equality|equal]] to $\mathcal{S}$ [^1] 
$$
A \subset \mathcal{S}: A \subseteq \mathcal{S} \text{ and } A \neq \mathcal{S} 
$$
