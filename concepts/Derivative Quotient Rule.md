# Quotient Rule

## Definition

The [[Derivative]] of the factor of two differentiable [[Function|Functions]] $f(x), g(x)$ is defined as the following

$$
\frac{ d }{ dx } \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}
= \frac{{\frac{d f }{ dx}g(x) - \frac{d g }{ dx}f(x)}}{(g(x))^2}
$$
