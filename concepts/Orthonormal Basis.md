# Orthonormal Basis

## Statement

Consider an $n$-dimensional [[Vector Space]] $V$ and [[Basis]] $B = \{ b_1, \ldots, b_n \}$ of $V$.

If the following holds then the basis is an [[Orthonormal Basis]]

$$
\begin{cases}
	\langle b_i, b_j \rangle = 0 & i \neq j \\
	\langle b_i, b_i \rangle = 1
\end{cases}
$$
