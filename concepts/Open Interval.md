# Open Interval

## Definition

An [[Open Interval]] $(a, b)$ is the [[Set]] of [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] between [[Real Number|Real Numbers]] $a, b$, not including these bounds [^1]

$$
[a, b] = \{ x \in \mathbb{R}: a \lt x \lt b \}
$$
