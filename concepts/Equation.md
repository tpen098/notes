## Definition

An [[Equation]] is a statement that two [[concepts/Expression|Expressions]] are equal [^1]

$$
\text{expression 1} = \text{expression 2}
$$

## Solution

The values of the [[Variable|Variables]] in the [[Equation|Equations]] that make the equality true are called the [[Equation|Roots]] or [[Equation|Solutions]] of the [[Equation]]. [^1]
