# Relationship Set Degree

## Definition

The [[Relationship Set Degree]] is the number of [[Entity Set|Entity Sets]] in [[Relationship|Participation]] of a [[Relationship Set]] [^1]

> [!INFO] Common Degrees
> An example would be [[Binary Relationship Set|Binary Relationship Sets]] that have a degree of 2 and [[Ternary Relationship Set|Ternary Relationship Sets]] that have a degree of 3.
