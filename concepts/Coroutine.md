# Coroutine

## Definition

A [[Coroutine]] is a special kind of [[Subprogram]] wherein the caller and the [[Subprogram]] are more equitable rather than a manager-worker dynamic. They have multiple entry points controlled by the [[Coroutine|Coroutines]] themselves, requiring them to be history sensitive and use [[Local]] [[Static Variable|Static Variables]] [^1]

> [!Trivia] Invocation
> The invocation of a [[Coroutine]] is called **Resume** rather than call due to it often starting at points other than its beginning
