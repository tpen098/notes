# Eigen Spectrum

## Definition

For a [[Square Matrix]] $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, the set of all [Eigenvalues](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) of $A$ is the called [[Eigen Spectrum]] or just [[Eigen Spectrum|Spectrum]].
