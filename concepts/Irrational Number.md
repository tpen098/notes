## Definition

The [[Irrational Number|Set of Irrational Numbers]] is the [[Set]] consisting of numbers $r$ that cannot be expressed as a ratio of [[Integer|Integers]]

$$
\mathbb{I} =  \left\{  q: q \neq \frac{m}{n}, m, n \in \mathbb{Z}, n \neq 0 \right\}
$$
