# Cartesian Product

## Definition

The [[Cartesian Product]] of [[Set|Sets]] $\mathcal{S}_{1}, \mathcal{S}_{2}, \dots, \mathcal{S}_{n}$ is the [[Set Collection]] $\mathcal{S}_{1} \times \mathcal{S}_{2} \times \dots \times \mathcal{S}_{n}$ consisting of all possible [[Tuple|Tuples]] $(s_{1}, s_{2}, \dots, s_{n})$ containing an [[Set Membership|Element]] for each of the [[Set|Sets]] [^1]

$$
\mathcal{S}_{1} \times \mathcal{S}_{2} \times \dots \times \mathcal{S}_{n}
= \{ (s_{1}, s_{2}, \dots, s_{n}) | s_{1} \in \mathcal{S}_{1}, s_{2} \in\mathcal{S}_{2}, \dots, s_{n} \in \mathcal{S}_{n}  \}
$$
