# Probability

## Definition

With each [[Event Space|Event]] in the [[Event Space]] $A \in \mathcal{A}$, a [[Probability]] or [[Function]] is associated $P(A)$ that measures the degree of belief that an [[Event Space|Event]] will occur.

### Output Ranges

The [[Codomain]] of the [[Probability]] is the decimal range $[0, 1]$

> [!Note] Probability of the Sample Space
> The [[Probability]] of the [[Sample Space]] is $P(\Omega)=1$
