# Physical Data Independence

## Definition

[[Physical Data Independence]] describes the independence of the [[Database-Management System#Physical Level|Physical Level]]'s implementation with the [[Database-Management System#Logical Level|Logical Level]] or [[Database-Management System#View Level|View Level]] . [^1]
