# Regularization

## Definition

The [[Regularization|Regularizer]] prevents the overfitting of a [[Machine Learning Model|Model]] or its incapacity to generalize its finding in an initial training [[Dataset]] to a new, test or application [[Dataset]]. [^1]

## Examples

Examples of [[Regularization]] included weight penalty, adding noise, inductive bias on the [[Dataset]], sudden dropout of weights mid-training, etc. [^1]
