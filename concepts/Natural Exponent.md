# Natural Exponent

## Definition

The [[Natural Exponent]] is an [[Exponent|Exponential]] [[Expression]] with the [[Exponent|Base]] being [[Euler's Number]] $e$ and a constant [[Exponent]] $x$

$$
e^{x}
$$
