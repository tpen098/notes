# Mapping Cardinality

## Definition

A [[Mapping Cardinality]] expresses the number of [[Entity|Entities]] to which another [[Entity]] can be associated via a [[Relationship Set]] [^1]

> [!NOTE] Directions
> A directed line $\rightarrow$ is used to signify an [[Entity]] can only be associated at most with one [[Entity]] in another [[Entity Set]], with the arrow pointing away from the limited [[Entity Set]]. Conversely, a straight line is used to signify an [[Entity]] can be associated with more than one [[Entity]]. [^1]
