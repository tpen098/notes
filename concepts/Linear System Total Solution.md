# Linear System Total Solution

## Definition

The [[Linear System Total Solution|Total Solution]] of a [[Linear System]] is the sum of its [[Linear System Particular Solution]] and [[Linear System General Solution]].

$$
x \in \mathbb{R}^n,
\lambda_i \in \mathbb{R}:
x=
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{p1} \\ x_{p2} \\ \vdots\\ x_{pm}
\end{bmatrix}
\lambda_1
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{11} \\ x_{12} \\ \vdots \\ x_{1m}
\end{bmatrix}
\lambda_2
\begin{bmatrix}
	x_{21} \\ x_{22} \\ \vdots\\ x_{2m}
\end{bmatrix}
\ldots
$$
