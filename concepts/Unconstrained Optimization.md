# Unconstrained Optimization

## Definition

Given a real-valued [[Function]] $f: \mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}$, [[Unconstrained Optimization]] is defined as the minimization of the [[Function]] $f$ with respect to its arguments $\theta$. [^1]

$$
\min_{\theta} f(\theta)
$$
