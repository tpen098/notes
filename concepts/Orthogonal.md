# Orthogonal

## Statement

Two [[Vector Space|Vectors]], $x, y$ are [[Orthogonal]] if and only if their [[Inner Product]] is zero.

$$
x \bot y:\langle x, y \rangle = 0
$$

> [!NOTE] General Concept of Perpendicularity
> [[Orthogonal|Orthogonality]] is the generalization of the concept of perpendicularity to bilinear forms that do not have to be the [[Dot Product]]

> [!tip]
> The $\bf{0}$-vector is [[Orthogonal]] to every vector in the [[Vector Space]]

## Example

$$
\langle \sin{x}, \cos{x} \rangle |_{-\pi}^{+\pi}
= \int_{-\pi}^{+\pi} \sin{x} \cos{x} = 0
$$
