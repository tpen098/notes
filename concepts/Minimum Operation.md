## Definition

The [[Minimum Operation]] $MINIMUM(S)$ is a [[Query Operation]] on a [[Total Order|Totally Ordered]] [[Data Structure]] that returns a [[concepts/Pointer]] to the smallest [[Set Membership|Element]] $x$ [^1]
