# Marginal Probability Density Function

## Definition

Given [[Random Variable|Random Variables]] $X = \{ X_{1}, \dots, X_{n} \} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, of a Continuous [[Target Space]], the _Marginal [[Probability Density Function]]_ of $X_{i}$ with state $x_i \in \mathcal{T}_{i}$ is defined as the [[Probability]] of $a_{i} \leq X_{i} \leq b_{i}$ irrespective of the other [[Random Variable|Random Variables]]

$$
P(a_{i} \leq X_{i} \leq b_{i})
$$
