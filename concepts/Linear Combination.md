# Linear Combination

## Statement

---

Consider a [[Vector Space]] $V$ and a finite number of [[Vector Space|Vectors]] $x_1, \ldots, x_k \in V$. Then for every $v \in V$ with the following form is a [[Linear Combination]] of the aforementioned [[Vector Space|Vectors]].

$$
v = \sum_{i=1}^k
	\lambda_i x_i \in V, \lambda_i \in \mathbb{R}
$$
