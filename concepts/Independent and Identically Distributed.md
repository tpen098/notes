# Independent and Identically Distributed

## Definition

It is common to assume for [[Dataset|Datasets]] that they are [[Independent and Identically Distributed]]. This means that any two data points $(x_{i}, y_{i}), (x_{j}, y{j})$ are [[Statistical Independence|Independent]] of each other and that the [[Empirical Mean]] is a good estimate of its [[Mean]].
