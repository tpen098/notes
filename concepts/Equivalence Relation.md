## Definition

A [[Binary Relation]] is an [[Equivalence Relation]] if it is [[Reflexive Relation|Reflexive]], [[Symmetric Relation|Symmetric]], and [[Transitive Relation|Transitive]] [^1]

> [!example]
> An example of an [[Equivalence Relation]] is the operator $=$ (by elimination from the [[Symmetric Relation]])
