# Isomorphism

## Definition

An [[Isomorphism]] is a [[Homomorphism]] that has the [[Bijective Property]] [^1]

$$
\Phi: V \rightarrow W
$$

### Theorem

Finite-dimensional [[Vector Space|vector spaces]] $V, W$ are isomorphic if and only if their [[Dimension]] is the same [^1]

$$ dim(V) = dim(W) $$

> [!Note]
> Any $n$-dimensional vector space is isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^n$
