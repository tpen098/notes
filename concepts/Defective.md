# Defective

## Definition

A [[Square Matrix]] is [[Defective]] if it possesses fewer than $n$ [Linearly Independent](Linear%20Independence.md) [Eigenvectors](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md).

> [!warning]
> A non-defective matrix does not necessarily require $n$ distinct [Eigenvalues](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) but it does require that the [Eigenvectors](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^N$.

## Algebraic Multiplicity a Defective Matrix

A [[Defective]] [[Square Matrix]] has at least one [Eigenvalue](Eigenvalue%20Equation.md) $\lambda_i$ with an [Algebraic Multiplicity](Algebraic%20Multiplicity.md) $m > 1$

## Geometric Multiplicity of a Defective Matrix

A [[Defective]] [[Square Matrix]] has a [Geometric Multiplicity](Geometric%20Multiplicity.md) less than $m$.
