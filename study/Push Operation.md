# Push Operation

## Definition

The [[Push Operation]] $PUSH(S, x)$ or [[Push Operation|Enqueue Operation]] $ENQUEUE(S, x)$ is a specific [[Insert Operation]] wherein an [[Set Membership|Element]] $x$ is inserted at the relative edge or end of a [[Data Structure]] [^1]
