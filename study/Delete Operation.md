# Delete Operation

## Definition

The [[Delete Operation]] $DELETE(S, x)$ is a [[Modifying Operation]] that, given a [[Pointer]] to an [[Set Membership|Element]] $x$ in the [[Dynamic Set]] $S$, removes $x$ from $S$ [^1]
