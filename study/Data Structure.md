# Data Structure

## Definition

A [[Data Structure]] is the implementation or realization of an [[Abstract Data Type]] in a [[Language|Programming Language]] in terms of the language's [[Data Type]] or other [[Data Structure|Data Structures]] such that operations on the [[Data Structure]] are expressible in terms of directly executable procedures. [^1]

> [!tldr] TLDR
> The [[Data Type]] represents basic building blocks of data. The combination or collection of these [[Data Type|Data Types]] created [[Data Structure|Data Structures]], that follow the design of an [[Abstract Data Type]].

A [[Data Structure]] is a way to store and organize a [[Set Collection|Collection]] of data for facilitating [[Query Operation|Query Operations]] and [[Modifying Operation|Modifying Operations]] [^2]
