# Image

## Definition

The [[Image]] of a [[Set]] $A' \subseteq A$ under the [[Function]] $f$ is defined as the [[Subset]] of the [[Function|Codomain]] wherein all [[Set Membership|Elements]] of $A'$ is given to the [[Function]] to produce the resulting [[Set]] $f(A')$ [^1]

$$
f(A') = \{ b \in B: b = f(a) \text{ for some } a \in A' \}
$$
