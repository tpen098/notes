# Symmetric Matrix

## Definition

A [[Square Matrix]] is a [[Symmetric Matrix]] if the [[Matrix]] is equal to its [[Matrix Transpose]].

$$A = A^{\top}$$

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
	a_{d1} & a_{v2} & a_{v3} & a_{v4} & \ldots & a_{vn}
	\\
	a_{v2} & a_{d2} & a_{s3} & a_{sb4} &\ldots & a_{w2}
	\\
	a_{v3} & a_{s3} & a_{d3} & a_{sa4} &\ldots & a_{w3}
	\\
	a_{v4} & a_{sa4} & a_{sb4} & a_{d4} &\ldots & a_{w4}
	\\
	\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots &\ddots & \vdots
	\\
	a_{vn} & a_{w2} & a_{w3} & a_{w4} &\ldots & a_{dn}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
