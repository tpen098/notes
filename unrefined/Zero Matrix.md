# Zero Matrix

## Definition

A [[Zero Matrix]] $\bf{0}_{m \times n}$ is a [[Matrix]] wherein the all the elements are zero-valued.

$$
0_{m \times n} = \begin{bmatrix}
	0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\
	0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\
	\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
	0 & 0 & \ldots & 0
	\end{bmatrix}
$$
