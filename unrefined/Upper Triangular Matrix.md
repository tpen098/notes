# Upper Triangular Matrix

## Definition

An [[Upper Triangular Matrix]] is a [[Matrix]] wherein all elements to the left and below the diagonal components are zero.

$$
A = [a_{ij}]:
\begin{cases}
	 a_{ij} = 0 & i > j\\
\end{cases}
$$

$$
A_{m \times n} = \begin{bmatrix}
	a_{11} & a_{12} & a_{13} & \ldots & a_{1n} \\
	0 & a_{22} & a_{23} &\ldots & a_{2n} \\
	0 & 0 & a_{33} & \ldots & a_{3n} \\
	\vdots & \vdots & \ldots &\ddots & \vdots \\
	0 & 0 & 0 &\ldots & a_{mn}
	\end{bmatrix}
$$
