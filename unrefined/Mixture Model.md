# Mixture Model

## Definition

A [[Mixture Model]] is a finite combination of $K$ [[Probability|Probability Distributions]] within individual mixture weights $0 \geq \pi_{k} \geq 1$

$$
p(x) = \sum_{k=1}^{K} \pi_{k} p_{k} (x)
$$
