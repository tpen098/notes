# Uniform Distribution

## Definition

The [[Uniform Distribution]] is a [[Probability Density Function]] for a continuous [[Random Variable]] $X$ wherein its value is a constant $k$ if its within the given ranges $[a, b]$; Else, it's zero.

$$
p(x) =
\begin{cases}
	k & a \leq x \leq b \\
	0 & \text{otherwise} \\
\end{cases}
$$
